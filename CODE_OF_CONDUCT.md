# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This is a small project about deploying Windows machines; there is
nothing here worth being unpleasant to another person over.

## What that means

- Assume the other person is competent and acting in good faith. A question that
  looks obvious to you usually is not obvious from where they are standing.
- Critique the code, the idea or the documentation — never the person.
- "I do not understand this" is useful information about the project, not an
  admission of anything. Treat it as a documentation bug.
- Nobody here is paid for this. Nobody is owed a response on a schedule.

## Not welcome

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
That includes deliberately derailing a discussion, and it includes doing any of
it in private after meeting somebody here.

## If something goes wrong

Report it to the maintainer at anthonyboily2005@gmail.com. Reports are handled
privately. The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, close threads, and
block accounts, in whatever measure the situation calls for.

If the maintainer is the problem, say so plainly in the report — that is a fair
thing to raise.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and commit comments on this
repository, and to anywhere somebody is representing the project.
